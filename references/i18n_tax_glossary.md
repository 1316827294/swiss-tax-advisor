# Multilingual Swiss Tax Glossary (DE / FR / IT / EN)

Standardized tax terminology across Switzerland's national official languages and English.

| Domain / Concept | German (DE) | French (FR) | Italian (IT) | English (EN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Salary Certificate** | Lohnausweis | Certificat de salaire | Certificato di salario | Salary Certificate |
| **Tax Return** | Steuererklärung | Déclaration d'impôt | Dichiarazione delle imposte | Tax Return / Filing |
| **Tax Dossier** | Steuerdossier | Dossier fiscal | Dossier fiscale | Tax Dossier |
| **Taxable Income** | Steuerbares Einkommen | Revenu imposable | Reddito imponibile | Taxable Income |
| **Gross Income** | Bruttoeinkommen | Revenu brut | Reddito lordo | Gross Income |
| **Net Income** | Nettoeinkommen | Revenu net | Reddito netto | Net Income |
| **Direct Federal Tax** | Direkte Bundessteuer | Impôt fédéral direct (IFD) | Imposta federale diretta (IFD) | Direct Federal Tax |
| **Cantonal Tax** | Staatssteuer / Kantonssteuer | Impôt cantonal | Imposta cantonale | Cantonal Tax |
| **Communal Tax** | Gemeindesteuer | Impôt communal | Imposta comunale | Municipal / Communal Tax |
| **Wealth Tax** | Vermögenssteuer | Impôt sur la fortune | Imposta sulla sostanza | Wealth Tax |
| **Withholding Tax** | Quellensteuer | Impôt à la source | Imposta alla fonte | Withholding Tax |
| **Tax Multiplier** | Steuerfuss | Coefficient d'impôt | Moltiplicatore d'imposta | Tax Multiplier |
| **Pillar 3a** | Säule 3a | 3e pilier A | 3° pilastro A | Pillar 3a (Tied Pension) |
| **Pension Fund (BVG)** | Pensionskasse (BVG) | Caisse de pension (LPP) | Cassa pensione (LPP) | Occupational Pension (2nd Pillar) |
| **Voluntary Buy-In** | Einkauf 2. Säule | Rachat 2e pilier | Riscatto 2° pilastro | Voluntary Pension Buy-In |
| **Commute Expenses** | Fahrkosten (S1) | Frais de déplacement | Spese di trasporto | Commute Expenses |
| **Meal Allowance** | Verpflegung (S2) | Frais de repas | Spese di vitto | Meal Expenses |
| **Medical Expenses** | Krankheitskosten (S3) | Frais de maladie | Spese per malattia | Illness & Healthcare Costs |
| **Childcare Costs** | Drittbetreuung (S6) | Frais de garde | Spese di custodia | Third-Party Childcare Costs |
| **Education & Training**| Weiterbildung (S7) | Perfectionnement | Perfezionamento | Continuing Education |
| **Property Maintenance**| Liegenschaftsunterhalt (S8) | Entretien d'immeubles | Manutenzione immobili | Property Maintenance |
| **Home Office** | Arbeitszimmer (S9) | Bureau à domicile | Studio a domicilio | Private Study / Home Office |
| **Custody Fees** | Depotgebühren (S10) | Droits de garde | Diritti di custodia | Securities Custody Fees |
| **Debt Interest** | Schuldzinsen (S11) | Intérêts passifs | Interessi passivi | Debt & Mortgage Interest |
| **Donations** | Spenden (S12) | Dons | Donazioni | Charitable Donations |
| **Dual-Earner Deduction**| Zweitverdienerabzug | Déduction pour double gain | Deduzione doppio reddito | Dual-Earner Deduction |
| **Filing Extension** | Fristerstreckung | Prolongation de délai | Proroga del termine | Filing Deadline Extension |
| **Audit Risk Radar** | Steuerrisiko-Radar | Radar de risque fiscal | Radar di rischio fiscale | Tax Audit Risk Radar |
| **Flat-Rate Deduction** | Pauschalabzug | Déduction forfaitaire | Deduzione forfettaria | Flat-Rate Deduction |
| **Marriage Penalty** | Heiratsstrafe | Pénalité de mariage | Penalità fiscale sul matrimonio | Marriage Tax Penalty |
| **Tax Assessment** | Steuerveranlagung | Taxation | Tassazione | Tax Assessment Order |
