# Swiss Tax Deduction Taxonomy (Comprehensive Full-Spectrum Edition)

This document defines the unified deduction taxonomy across Swiss Federal Direct Tax (DBG / LIFD), Cantonal Tax Harmonization (StHG / LHID), and the official Cantonal benchmark specifications (including **Canton Bern 2025/2026 Master Guidelines**).

---

## 1. Professional Expenses (Berufsauslagen / Frais professionnels)

### S1: Commute Expenses (Fahrkosten / Frais de déplacement)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 26 Abs. 1 a / StHG Art. 9 Abs. 1.
- **Rule**:
  - **Public Transit**: Fully deductible up to cantonal cap (GA, train tickets).
  - **Private Car**: 0.70 CHF/km. Permitted only if no viable transit exists or public transit takes >= 60 minutes longer per day.
  - **Bicycle / E-Bike**: Flat rate of **CHF 700 / year**.
- **Statutory Caps**:
  - Federal: **CHF 3,300** max.
  - Canton Bern (BE): **CHF 7,000** max.
  - Canton Zürich (ZH): **CHF 5,000** max.

### S2: Meal Allowance (Auswärtige Verpflegung / Frais de repas)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 26 Abs. 1 b / Berufskostenverordnung Art. 11.
- **Rule**:
  - **Standard Flat Rate**: **CHF 3,200 / year** (CHF 15 / day) if unable to return home for lunch.
  - **Subsidized Canteen Rate**: **CHF 1,600 / year** (CHF 7.50 / day) if employer canteen or lunch vouchers provided (marked in Lohnausweis Box 13/G).

### S7: Continuing Education & Training (Weiterbildung / Perfectionnement)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 j / StHG Art. 9 Abs. 2.
- **Statutory Cap**: **CHF 12,700 / year** (Federal and most cantons).
- **Rule**: Must be professionally oriented (berufsorientiert). Excludes hobby courses. Requires invoices and employer job-relevance letter.

### S9: Private Home Office / Study (Arbeitszimmer / Bureau à domicile)
- **Legal Basis**: Swiss Supreme Court precedent BGE 140 II 157.
- **Rule**:
  - Only allowed if employer does **not** provide a suitable workstation and >= 40% WFH is required.
  - **Conflict Caveat**: If home office rent is claimed for certain days, commute (S1) and meal allowances (S2) cannot be claimed for those same days!
  - Formula: $\text{Annual Rent} / (\text{Rooms} + 2) \times (\text{WFH Days}/5) \times 12$.

### S23: Professional Clothing & Specialized Tools (Berufskleidung & Werkzeuge)
- **Legal Basis**: StG BE Art. 25 Abs. 1 c.
- **Rule**: Only genuine safety clothing or specialized trade tools (e.g. steel-toe boots, sterile medical attire, professional chef knives) can be deducted. Ordinary business suits/office wear are strictly non-deductible.

## 2. Pension, Insurance & Social Security (Vorsorge & Versicherung)

### 1.1 / S4: Tied Pension Pillar 3a (Säule 3a / 3e pilier A)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 e / BVV 3.
- **Caps (2025/2026)**:
  - Employed with 2nd Pillar: **CHF 7,258.00**.
  - Self-employed without 2nd Pillar: **20% of net earned income**, up to **CHF 36,288.00**.

### M1 / S5: 2nd Pillar Voluntary Buy-in (Pensionskasse Einkauf / Rachat BVG)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 d / BVG Art. 9.
- **Rule**: Fully deductible in payment year. Note: 3-year lock before lump-sum retirement withdrawal.

### M16 / S20: Self-Employed 2nd Pillar Contributions (Selbständigerwerbende BVG)
- **Rule**: Only the personal employee contribution share and personal buy-ins can be deducted on the private tax return. The employer share must be expensed in business accounts.

### 4.2: Health & Life Insurance Flat Deduction (Versicherungsabzug)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 g / StG BE Art. 37.
- **Bern Statutory Caps (2025)**:
  - Single with 2nd Pillar: **CHF 2,400**. Without 2nd Pillar: **CHF 3,600**.
  - Married with 2nd Pillar: **CHF 4,800**. Without 2nd Pillar: **CHF 7,200**.
  - Per Child: **CHF 700**.

## 3. Family, Childcare & Maintenance (Familie & Alimente)

### 2.1a / S3: Dual-Earner Deduction (Zweitverdienerabzug / Double gain)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 2 / StG BE Art. 40.
- **Federal**: 50% of the lower spouse's earned income (Min CHF 8,600, Max CHF 14,100).
- **Canton Bern**: 2% of total joint earned income (Min CHF 1,000, Max CHF 2,600).

### 2.1b / S6: Third-Party Childcare Costs (Drittbetreuungskosten / Kita)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 3 / StG BE Art. 39.
- **Statutory Cap**: Up to **CHF 25,500 / child / year** (Federal & Bern). Must attach official Kita/daycare invoices.

### S4: Out-of-Home Education Deduction (Kinder in auswärtiger Ausbildung)
- **Legal Basis**: StG BE Art. 35 Abs. 1 c (Canton Bern specific).
- **Rule**: Extra deduction (up to CHF 6,000) for parents paying lodging/transport costs when a child pursues their first post-secondary educational degree outside the daily commute radius.

### M6 / S19: Alimony & Maintenance Payments (Alimente / Pensions alimentaires)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 c / StG BE Art. 36.
- **CRITICAL LEGAL DISTINCTION**:
  - Alimony paid to **former spouses** and **minor children**: **100% Tax Deductible** for the payer (taxable for recipient).
  - Financial support paid to **adult children (18+)**: **Strictly NON-DEDUCTIBLE** as alimony! (Only eligible for standard Unterstützungsabzug if poverty conditions are proven).

### 5.2 / S5: Support of Incapacitated Persons (Unterstützungsabzug)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 35 Abs. 1 b.
- **Rule**: Up to **CHF 6,500** per dependent if taxpayer provides at least CHF 3,200/year to an incapacitated relative (excluding spouse and minor children).

## 4. Property, Energy & Building Maintenance (Grundstückskosten)

### M4 / M5: Real Estate Maintenance (Actual vs. Pauschal)
- **Rule**: Choose between **Actual evidenced expenses (M4)** or **Statutory Flat Rate (M5)**:
  - Flat rate: 10% of gross rental value for buildings <= 10 years old; 20% for buildings > 10 years old.

### M10 / S15: Energy Saving & Environmental Renovations (Energiesparmassnahmen)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 32 Abs. 2.
- **Rule**: Insulation, window upgrades, heat pump conversions are 100% deductible even if value-increasing.
- **Tax Loss Carryforward**: If renovation expenses exceed net taxable income in the invoice year, excess costs can be carried forward over the **next 2 consecutive tax years**!

### M11 / S22: Photovoltaic & Solar Thermal Installations (PV-Anlagen 2024 Rule)
- **Rule**: Under updated legislation since 2024, solar installations on both existing and newly constructed residential buildings are deductible and eligible for the 2-year carryforward.

### M13 / S21: Renovation Fund Allocations (Erneuerungsfonds)
- **Rule**: Contributions into a co-ownership property renewal fund are deductible **in the year paid**, provided the fund is exclusively designated for common infrastructure maintenance.

### M14 / S16: Demolition Costs for Replacement Construction (Rückbaukosten)
- **Rule**: Demolition costs incurred when replacing an old dwelling with an equivalent energy-efficient new building are deductible as property maintenance.

### M15 / S18: Vacant Room Under-Utilization (Unternutzungsabzug)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 21 Abs. 2 (Federal Direct Tax ONLY, not applicable for Bern cantonal tax).
- **Rule**: If rooms in an owner-occupied house become permanently unoccupied (e.g. after children move out or spouse passes away), taxable deemed rental value (Eigenmietwert) is reduced by a ratio.

## 5. Health, Disability & Hardship (Krankheit & Behinderung)

### S3: Healthcare & Dental Out-of-Pocket (Krankheitskosten)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 h.
- **Rule**: Only deductible to the extent expenses exceed **5% of annual net income**.

### M9 / S7: Disability-Related Expenses (Behinderungsbedingte Kosten)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 h-bis / Behindertenkostenverordnung.
- **CRITICAL DISTINCTION**:
  - Unlike S3 medical bills, disability-related expenses (wheelchairs, home accessibility ramps, special transport) have **ZERO 5% threshold**! They are 100% deductible from the first Swiss Franc.

## 6. Financial, Capital & Debt (Vermögensverwaltung & Zinsen)

### M2 / S11: Private Debt & Mortgage Interest (Schuldzinsen)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 a.
- **Rule**: Private mortgage and loan interest is deductible up to gross investment income + CHF 50,000.
- **Caveat**: Credit card penalty fees and consumer leasing installments are **NOT** deductible.

### M3 / S10: Securities Custody Fees (Depotgebühren)
- **Legal Basis**: DBG Art. 32 Abs. 1.
- **Rule**: Bank safekeeping fees and official tax statement costs (Steuerverzeichnis) are deductible. Tax advisor fees and broker trading commissions are non-deductible.

## 7. Automated Baseline Reductions (Automatic Tax Reductions)

### 1.2 / S1: Single-Parent Household Deduction (Haushaltabzug / Monoparental)
- **Bern 2025**: **CHF 2,400** for single parents living with minor or dependent children in their household.

### AUTO / S2: Low & Middle Income Relief (Minderbelastungsabzug)
- **Bern 2025**: Automatically computed by TaxMe tax authorities for net incomes under CHF 25,000, tapering off gradually.
