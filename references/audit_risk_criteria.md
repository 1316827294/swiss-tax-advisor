# Swiss Cantonal Tax Administration Audit Screening Criteria (Risikobasierte Fallauswahl)

Swiss cantonal tax administrations (Steuerverwaltung / Administration fiscale) do not examine every declaration manually. They deploy automated heuristic screening systems (**Risikobasierte Fallauswahl**) that automatically flag returns showing anomalous statistical deviations.

Returns with high risk scores trigger intensive manual audits with formal demands for documentation (**Auflage / Demande de pièces justificatives**).

---

## 1. Automated Red-Flag Thresholds

### Risk Rule 1: High Continuing Education Ratio (S7 Weiterbildung)
- **Trigger**: Declared S7 costs exceed **15% of annual net income** or exceed **CHF 8,000**.
- **Audit Action**: Auditor demands proof that courses directly maintain or improve qualifications in the current professional occupation (DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 j).
- **Defense Strategy**: Prepare official receipts from accredited institutions + employer confirmation letter (Arbeitgeberbestätigung) confirming direct relevance to daily job duties.

### Risk Rule 2: Car Commute in High-Density Public Transit Areas (S1 Fahrkosten)
- **Trigger**: Taxpayer declares private car at 0.70 CHF/km while home and work addresses are directly served by SBB rail or urban transit with under 60 minutes time difference.
- **Audit Action**: Auditor reduces deduction from car calculation (e.g. CHF 6,000) down to the 2nd class General Abonnement (GA) or relevant Streckenabo price (approx. CHF 3,995 or lower).
- **Defense Strategy**: Provide shift work rosters (early morning / night shifts before trains operate), medical certificates, or a detailed route time log showing >= 60 min difference.

### Risk Rule 3: Home Office without Proof of Necessity (S9 Arbeitszimmer)
- **Trigger**: Rent deduction claimed for private study room when taxpayer is an employee.
- **Audit Action**: Auditor checks Lohnausweis Field 15. If employer provides a permanent workstation, 100% of the deduction is denied under Supreme Court case BGE 140 II 157.
- **Defense Strategy**: Secure an official letter from employer HR stating that no fixed desk is assigned and telecommuting is compulsory.

### Risk Rule 4: Claimed Deductions Exceeding CHF 500 without Scanned Receipts
- **Trigger**: Itemized deductions claimed without attached proof or marked as missing receipts.
- **Audit Action**: Total disallowance of unsubstantiated amounts.
- **Defense Strategy**: Never file unevidenced expenses. Where receipts are lost, switch to statutory flat deductions (Pauschalabzug).

### Risk Rule 5: Value-Adding vs Value-Maintaining Property Expenses (S8 Liegenschaft)
- **Trigger**: Claiming luxury additions (e.g. heated swimming pool, luxury sauna installation) under ordinary real estate maintenance.
- **Audit Action**: Reclassification to non-deductible investment expenditures (wertvermehrend).
- **Defense Strategy**: Keep detailed contractor invoices separating pure repairs/maintenance (werterhaltend) from energy-saving components.

### Risk Rule 6: Full SBB GA Claimed for Short or Intra-Commune Commute (S1 Fahrkosten)
- **Trigger**: Taxpayer claims the full SBB GA (CHF 3,995), but residence (Wohnort) and workplace (Arbeitsort) are in the same municipality or neighboring zones where local transit (e.g., Libero, ZVV, Unireso, Passepartout) costs significantly less (CHF 750–1,200).
- **Audit Action**: Cantonal auditor immediately disallows the excess and cuts the deduction down to the necessary local network pass (e.g. Bern local Libero ~CHF 790), reclassifying the difference as non-deductible private leisure travel (Privatanteil). Additionally, federal tax automatically caps at statutory limit (CHF 3,200).
- **Defense Strategy**: Check Lohnausweis Box 13G/15. Only claim full GA if point-to-point Streckenabo across cantons/regions exceeds GA cost, or if employer requires multi-site travel without reimbursement.
