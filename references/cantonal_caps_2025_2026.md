## 2. Indicative Marginal Tax Rates & Commute Caps

Marginal tax rate indicates the combined percentage of tax (Federal + Cantonal + Communal) saved on each additional 100 CHF deducted.

| Canton | Commute Cap (S1) | Typical Combined Marginal Tax Rate (Single, 100k-150k CHF) | Notes |
